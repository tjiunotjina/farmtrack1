What it keeps track of

Works offline
Everything saves to your phone first and syncs when signal returns. Add animals in the veld and they appear on your other devices later.
One record per animal
Ear tag, breed, birth date (age works itself out), photos, mother and offspring, and your stock brand.
Health history
Log vaccinations by name, record sickness and treatment, or vaccinate a whole species in one go.
Stock and feed
Set a daily feed rate and the stock count drops on its own. You get a low-stock warning before you run out.
Money tied to animals
Record sales and expenses against the animal or stock item they belong to, with a photo of the receipt.
Losses on the record
Mark animals sold, dead or lost, with the cause, so your herd count stays honest.
Start from your list
Paste or upload a list of ear tags and your herd is in the app in minutes.
Weather for your farm
Today's forecast, saved so you can still read it when you're offline.

Questions

Does it really work without signal?

Yes. Records are saved on your phone first, so adding an animal, logging a vaccination or recording a sale all work offline. Your changes sync the next time you have signal. You need a connection once, to create your account.

Do I need to install anything?

No app store needed. Open Orutumbo in your phone's browser and choose "Add to Home Screen". On an iPhone, use the Share button in Safari. It then opens like any other app.

What happens when the 30-day trial ends?

Your farm moves to the Free plan, which holds up to 15 animals. Nothing is deleted. If you have more animals than the Free plan allows, your existing records stay, and you can upgrade whenever you want to add more.

How do I pay for a paid plan?

For now, paid plans are activated after we confirm an EFT payment. Choose your plan inside the app and it shows you the payment details.

Who can see my farm's records?

Only people who log in to your farm's account. Every farm's records are kept separate from every other farm's.

Can my workers use it too?

You can keep a list of your farm team in the app, with their roles and phone numbers. Separate logins for each worker are not available yet.
